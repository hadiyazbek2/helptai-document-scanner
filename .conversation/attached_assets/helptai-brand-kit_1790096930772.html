Usage

Do and don't

Do

  • Give the mark clear space on all sides, roughly its own height
  • Use sage only for a completed or successful state
  • Keep amber small — a flag, a dot, a chip, not a background
  • Set body copy in Inter at 16px or larger for scanned-text-heavy screens

Don't

  • Recolor the mark outside its two defined variants
  • Use amber to signal an error or a destructive action
  • Stretch or condense the wordmark to fit a space
  • Set Lora below 18px — it was chosen for warmth, not density

Open before this kit is final: the name hasn't been trademark- or domain-checked yet; lowercase UI copy (buttons, labels) is a proposal to user-test, not a decision; the logo mark is a placeholder pending a proper designer pass.
